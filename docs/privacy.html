New Documents Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

New Documents creates empty files from Finder’s context menu. The app works entirely on your Mac and does not require an account or internet connection.

Data collection

New Documents does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs.

Files and settings

Your file-type preferences are stored locally in a shared container used by the app and its Finder extension. Folder permissions are stored locally as security-scoped bookmarks. These settings and permissions are not sent to the developer.

The extension asks you to select a folder before creating files there. You may select a parent folder to include its subfolders. Existing files are preserved, and new files receive a unique name.

Apple services

Apple handles App Store downloads and any diagnostics you choose to share through macOS under Apple’s privacy policy.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, open an issue in the project’s support tracker. Information you choose to post there is handled by GitHub and may be public. Do not include private files or sensitive information.
